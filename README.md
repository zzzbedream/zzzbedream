## ⚡ Thesis

**ZZZ BE DREAM SpA** is a DeepTech venture lab building base-layer infrastructure where three shifts of the next decade meet:

1. **Machine-to-machine settlement.** AI agents are becoming economic actors. They need credit, payment and delivery-provenance rails that are deterministic and verifiable on-chain, not trusted off-chain. → **Stellar / Soroban**
2. **Post-quantum and zero-knowledge sovereignty.** "Harvest now, decrypt later" breaks today's classical cryptography. Sensitive communication and state need NIST-standardized PQC combined with zero-knowledge proofs.
3. **Algorithmic physical infrastructure.** Solar generation and battery storage (**BESS**) are only as bankable as the price forecasts behind them. Probabilistic, calibrated market models turn grid volatility into a dispatch decision.
